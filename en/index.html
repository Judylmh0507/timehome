What You’re Buying

Around 320 pieces of American-style furniture, manufactured between 2009 and 2012, stored in a bonded warehouse ever since. The stock has never been opened, never displayed, never used, and we ship in original export cartons, exactly as they left the factory.

We are a reseller specialising in clearing this aged stock. We do not manufacture furniture and we are not the original factory or an OEM workshop — we simply hold this long-term warehouse inventory and sell it at clearance prices. Please do not assume “factory-direct” pricing or OEM origin; our prices reflect the fact that this is aged, clearance stock.

Materials — Be Aware

Construction varies by piece. Most items use a solid wood frame + quality real wood veneer + some engineered-board auxiliary materials. They are not 100% solid wood. Where relevant, the exact composition is listed on each product page. We describe materials honestly rather than labelling everything as “all solid wood”.

Possible Age / Storage Traces

Because the stock has been in long-term storage for many years, and based on our past sales experience, pieces may show minor age-related traces. These are cosmetic and surface-level at most, and in most cases do not affect the wood or structure. Common traces:

  • Aged / dusty cartons — original packaging may look old, with dust or storage marks.
  • Mildew / moisture marks — faint stains on the packaging from years in storage; the wood itself is typically unaffected.
  • Possible concealed bumps — hidden knocks or scuffs that may only appear once the carton is opened.

Before shipping, we can provide unpacking photos / videos of the actual piece on request.
