About TIMEHOME CLEARANCE

We are an independent reseller clearing a specific lot of long-term bonded-warehouse furniture. We are not the original factory, not an OEM workshop, and we do not manufacture furniture.

This stock was manufactured between 2009 and 2012, stored in original export cartons ever since, and is now being cleared at a fraction of the original price. Around 320 pieces remain; once sold, there is no replenishment.

We disclose condition honestly. Long storage may leave minor cosmetic traces on cartons or surfaces; materials are typically solid wood frame + real wood veneer, not 100% solid wood. Full details are on each product page.
